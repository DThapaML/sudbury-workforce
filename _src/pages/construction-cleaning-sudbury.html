Cleaning After Construction and Renovations

Construction dust settles on every surface, so we work from top to bottom: fixtures, cabinets, window tracks and finally the floors.

  • Sweeping and vacuuming construction dust
  • Wiping walls, trim, doors and baseboards
  • Cleaning inside and outside cabinets and drawers
  • Windows, sills and tracks
  • Kitchens and bathrooms after a renovation
  • Washing floors
  • Picking up and bagging leftover debris
  • A final clean before move-in or hand-over

Construction Cleaning Questions

When should I book construction cleaning?

Once the trades are finished and the dust has settled — usually right before move-in or hand-over. We can also do a rough clean between stages of a project.

Do you clean for contractors and builders?

Yes. We clean up after home renovations for homeowners, and for contractors and builders getting a space ready to hand over.

How is this different from regular house cleaning?

Construction dust gets into cabinets, window tracks and every corner, so it takes more detail than an everyday clean. For regular cleaning, see house and apartment cleaning.

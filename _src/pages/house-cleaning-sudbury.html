House Cleaning Questions

Do you clean apartments when tenants move out?

Yes. We clean apartments and houses before and after a move — handy for tenants handing back their keys and for landlords getting a unit ready for the next tenant.

Can you clean on a regular schedule?

Yes — weekly, every two weeks, monthly or just once. Pick what works for you and we'll plan around it.

What's included in a deep clean?

A deep clean goes past the everyday basics: inside cupboards, behind and under furniture, baseboards and door frames, and the built-up grime in kitchens and bathrooms.

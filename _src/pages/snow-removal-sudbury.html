Snow Removal Questions

Can you come back after every snowfall?

Yes. Book a one-time clear-out after a big storm, or ask us to come back after every snowfall this winter. Tell us what you need and we'll work out a plan with you.

Do you clear snow for seniors?

Yes. We shovel driveways, walkways and front steps for seniors across Greater Sudbury, after every snowfall or one storm at a time, so you never have to pick up a shovel.

How quickly can you get here?

We offer same-day service. Call or WhatsApp {{phone}} and we'll tell you when we can be there.

How much does snow removal cost?

It depends on the size of your driveway and walkways and how often you need us. Estimates are always free with no obligation — send us a few details or a photo and we'll get back to you fast.

Can you clear the snowbank the plow leaves behind?

Yes. That heavy, packed ridge at the end of the driveway is often the hardest part to shovel, and we clear it along with the rest of your driveway.

Which parts of Sudbury do you cover?

All of Greater Sudbury, including New Sudbury, the South End, Minnow Lake, Valley East, Chelmsford, Azilda, Lively, Garson and Coniston. See all the areas we serve, or read about snow removal in Hanmer & Valley East.
